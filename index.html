05 — Anatomy

Keep scrolling →

i.

Cold-gas thrusters.

Ten nozzles around the body vent compressed air. No combustion, no flame: the expanding air chills and fogs. Tesla says they push four to five times harder than the tires can grip.

ii.

A fan that holds on.

A fan in the floor pulls air out from under the car, so the atmosphere presses it onto whatever surface it's touching. Downforce at a standstill, and enough grip to drive upside down.

iii.

Three trims. One seat to fly.

Performance and Thrust carry two people. Rocket, the flight-ready one, carries just the driver, and it isn't approved for public roads.
